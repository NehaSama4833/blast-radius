# Demo script (about 2 minutes; fill real numbers after the benchmark)

1. **Hook (10 s).** "Six alerts at 3 AM. None of them is the problem." Show the map with red nodes spread everywhere.
2. **Break something (15 s).** Press *Surprise me*. Say: "I don't know what I just broke, and neither do the agents."
3. **Investigate (40 s).** Press *Investigate*. Narrate the lanes: Changes finds a deploy, Impact shows what would break and the revenue at risk, History finds the same pattern from a past incident, People picks who is awake. Point out that the culprit is an unmonitored database: no alert of its own.
4. **Proof (20 s).** Click the top hypothesis: the amber path lights up. "Every claim cites graph nodes. Invented citations are rejected by the server."
5. **Verdict and safety (20 s).** Show the simulated rollback: the alerts that clear turn green. "It never acts alone: a human presses Approve & post." Press it; show the Discord message.
6. **Memory (10 s).** Press *Mark resolved*. "Next time this pattern is recognised faster."
7. **Honesty beat (10 s).** Show the ambiguous scenario: it answers "undetermined" instead of guessing.
8. **Close (5 s).** Show the benchmark table: graph agents vs docs-only agents.
